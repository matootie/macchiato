macOS Menu Bar App

Keep your Mac
awake, effortlessly.

Macchiato lives quietly in your menu bar and toggles caffeinate with a single click — no open terminal window required.

Download for Mac Version 0.1.0 · macOS 14 Sonoma or later

Features

☕

One-click toggle

Start or stop caffeinate instantly from the menu bar. The icon updates to reflect the current state at a glance.

🪶

Zero footprint

No Dock icon, no floating windows. Macchiato stays completely out of your way until you need it.

🚀

Launch at login

Opt in to have Macchiato start automatically when you log in so it's always ready when you need it.

System Requirements: macOS 14 Sonoma or later · Apple Silicon or Intel
